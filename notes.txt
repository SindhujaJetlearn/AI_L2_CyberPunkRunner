Build a browser-based cyberpunk endless runner in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Use CSS
perspective to create a 3D skyline background with two parallax layers. The player
character jumps when I press Space, with gravity returning them to the ground. Double
jump allowed mid-air.

The Structural Block obstacle is colliding with the

more accurately.
